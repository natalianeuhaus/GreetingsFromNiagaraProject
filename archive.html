The Evidence

Built through four years of ongoing research, this archive brings together more than 800—and growing—government reports, historical radiological surveys, environmental investigations, legal records, photographs, news coverage, and other sources documenting Niagara’s industrial and nuclear history. The materials trace factories, workers, waste sites, waterways, contamination findings, and the regional network connected to the Manhattan Project and Cold War nuclear programs. The archive can also be searched through the Interactive Map, where many records are connected to specific locations and documented findings.
